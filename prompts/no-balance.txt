Mode no_balance: the account has no balance, and the test checks that trying to bet shows a message saying so.
- Before opening the game, the account balance must be zero or below the lowest stake a slot allows (0,10 €). If it is 0,10 € or more, this is the wrong account: do not open the game, and stop with status "test_failed" saying the no-balance account has balance.
- Press spin once at the minimum stake, and no more. Never deposit to get past the message, and close any deposit window it opens.
- After pressing, wait a few seconds and screenshot what the game shows. Copy the message text into the step that pressed spin, and set language to "es" when it is in Spanish, or to the message itself when it is not.

Expected criteria (all must pass for status "working"):
1. The game launches in real-money mode, with the empty account balance. If the lobby refuses to open the game and shows a message that the balance is not enough, that also counts: screenshot it and do not press anything else.
2. Trying to bet shows a clear message, in Spanish, that the balance is not enough (such as "Saldo insuficiente" or "Fondos insuficientes"), and no round is played.
3. Neither the Betsson balance nor the balance inside the game changes, and both are shown in euros.
If no message appears, the message is not in Spanish, a round is played anyway, a balance changes or is shown in another currency, the game freezes, or it shows an unrelated error, status is "failing". A block of the account, as described above, is "test_failed", not the refusal of criterion 1.

Replay script (no_balance): it reads balance_before in the lobby before anything else, the currency, the language of the message, and ends with an "expect" whose "matches" holds on the message that the balance is not enough, read from the page, a postMessage or a response. Read the language as the message's text when the page shows it, or as the language the game reports with it, such as the "lang" of its postMessage. If the lobby refused to open the game, the script has no spin and expects the lobby's message instead. If it spins, it reads the stake and game_balance_before before the spin, and balance_after, game_balance_after and the expect of the message after it: testson checks that neither balance changed. The replay presses spin only when the stake is above both balances, so that no round can be played.
