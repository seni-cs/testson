Mode real_money: the account has balance, and one round is played for real money.
- Before opening the game, the account balance must be above zero. If it is zero, stop with status "test_failed" and say the real-money account needs balance.
- Press spin exactly once, at the minimum stake. If the lowest stake the game allows is above 1 €, or above the balance, do not press it: stop with status "test_failed" and say why.
- If that one round awards free spins or a bonus, let the feature finish without paying for anything, then stop. Do not start a second round, even if the first one was rejected or did not settle.
- Wait until the round settles (reels stop, win or loss is shown) and the balance updates.

Expected criteria (all must pass for status "working"):
1. The game launches in real-money mode and shows the account balance.
2. One round at the minimum stake completes.
3. The Betsson balance changes by the stake and the win, if any, and so does the balance inside the game.
4. The balances and the stake are shown in euros.
If the game itself does not launch (a block of the account, as described above, is "test_failed"), the round errors or never settles, a balance is wrong, or the currency is not the euro, status is "failing".

Replay script (real_money): it reads balance_before in the lobby before anything else, the currency, game_balance_before and the stake before the spin, and the win, balance_after and game_balance_after once the round settled. Whether this round won is chance, and a replay plays another round, so always read the win, before the balances, with a pattern that also holds when the round won nothing, such as "tw=([\d.,]+)" in the response to the spin. testson checks that each balance after is its balance before less the stake plus the win, to the cent. Without a reading of the win, it can only check that each lost no more than the stake. Read balance_after from Betsson, such as the balance its page or its wallet shows, and game_balance_after from the game, such as the balance its server returns after the spin.
