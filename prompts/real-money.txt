Mode real_money: the account has balance, and one round is played for real money.
- Before opening the game, the account balance must be above zero. If it is zero, stop with status "test_failed" and say the real-money account needs balance.
- Press spin exactly once, at the minimum stake. If the lowest stake the game allows is above 1 €, or above the balance, do not press it: stop with status "test_failed" and say why.
- If that one round awards free spins or a bonus, let the feature finish without paying for anything, then stop. Do not start a second round, even if the first one was rejected or did not settle.
- Wait until the round settles (reels stop, win or loss is shown) and the balance updates.

Expected criteria (all must pass for status "working"):
1. The game launches in real-money mode and shows the account balance.
2. One round at the minimum stake completes, and the balance changes by the stake and the win, if any.
If the game does not launch, the round errors or never settles, or the balance is wrong, status is "failing".
